Feature showcase

Practical features for everyday use.

From local encrypted storage to system-level autofill, FAEVault delivers complete password management for Android and Windows.

Encrypted Vault

scrypt + AES-256-GCM encryption. Passwords and passkeys stay in a local file, fully under your control.

System Autofill

Integrated with Android Autofill framework. Fill credentials and OTPs with one tap in apps and websites.

Passkeys

Android 14+ Passkey Credential Provider. Create and verify passkeys for passwordless, fast authentication.

OCR Scanning

Scan credit cards and IDs via camera or gallery. Auto-extract card numbers, expiry dates, and personal details.

Security Guard

Anti-screenshot with FLAG_SECURE, 60-second auto-clear clipboard, exponential brute-force lockout, sensitive data re-verification.

Encrypted Sync

WebDAV for NAS/Nextcloud, LAN direct sync, encrypted backup export. Keep data consistent across devices.

Why it feels trustworthy

No ads · Free · Security you can see.

FAEVault contains no ads and all core features are free. Data is encrypted locally with scrypt + AES-256-GCM, never touching external servers. The encryption design and security architecture are transparent and regularly reviewed.
